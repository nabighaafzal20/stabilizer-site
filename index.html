01Precision Output Regulation

Regulated to
your target.

Set the output anywhere between 180 V and 235 V. The converter bucks the higher mains supply down to it and holds it there, with a live stability readout showing how tightly.

  • Target 180–235 V
  • Presets 200 · 210 · 220 · 230
02Transformer-less AC-AC Conversion

No mains transformer.
Just switching.

A switching stage works on the AC supply directly and steps it down to the target voltage, so regulation happens in the power electronics instead of in a heavy iron-core transformer.

  • AC → AC
  • Buck
  • 50 Hz nominal
